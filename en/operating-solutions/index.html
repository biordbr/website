Operational efficiency for food service

Increase Your Restaurant Margin by Turning Waste into Operating Credit

Restaurants, cafés, food service operations and commercial kitchens are looking for practical ways to reduce costs, improve operational efficiency and increase profitability without compromising customer experience.

Biord transforms used cooking oil into real financial value. Instead of treating that waste as a disposal cost, the operation creates balance in the platform that can be withdrawn by PIX or spent in the Biord Marketplace to access essential business tools.

Estimate your credit potential

The Biord profit model

Restaurant managers do not need to see waste only as a logistics problem. They need to see it as an opportunity to improve cash flow, efficiency and margin.

Biord connects used oil generation to a market model with transaction records, digital management and partner access. The restaurant receives real balance in the account, which can be used in two ways:

  • Immediate withdrawal by PIX: direct conversion of waste into usable cash value.
  • Marketplace use: trade balance for business tools and operational solutions.

This turns a disposal cost into a direct operational and financial advantage.

How this helps reduce costs and improve margin

Restaurant owners and operations managers are constantly looking for better ways to:

  • Reduce restaurant costs without compromising service quality or productivity.
  • Increase restaurant profit margin through tighter control of cash flow and cost structure.
  • Attract customers to the restaurant with stronger operational execution and better guest experience.
  • Monetize commercial waste with a transparent, auditable and digital process.

Biord offers a simple route to turn waste handling into a stronger operational performance model.

Marketplace of partner solutions

The balance generated from collected oil can be used inside the Biord ecosystem to access strategic partners that help restaurants operate better, grow revenue and improve cost control.

Zupy

Zupy helps restaurants attract customers, improve retention and create loyalty-driven campaigns that increase recurring revenue. It strengthens customer relationship and sales conversion.

ContaClara

ContaClara delivers specialized financial management for food service operations. With reconciliation, expense control and stronger cash visibility, managers protect the margin and improve planning.

EstocaAi

EstocaAi improves inventory control, reduces waste and supports better replenishment decisions. In food service, that means fewer losses, less excess and stronger operational efficiency.

Compliance and legal protection

The restaurant also gains operational protection through the automatic issuance of environmental compliance certificates. This reduces risk and helps prevent issues related to improper disposal, incomplete records and regulatory pressure.

In short, the operation gains legal protection and greater financial security in a process many companies treat as unavoidable expense.
