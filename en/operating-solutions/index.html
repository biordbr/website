How to Attract Customers, Run Promotions and Increase Restaurant Sales with Biord

Free local promotion to thousands of nearby residents. Biord turns your restaurant’s used cooking oil into a growth opportunity. Gain free local exposure in public spaces and residential communities, and use your balance to retain customers without expensive advertising.

Grow My Restaurant Sales

How Can You Promote Your Restaurant Without Paying for Ads?

Biord installs strategic Voluntary Drop-off Points (PEVs) in large residential communities and public squares. When residents drop off household cooking oil, they earn points and are directed to redeem rewards exclusively at local partner restaurants. By recycling with us, your business gains a local marketing showcase and reaches potential customers who already live nearby.

Turn an Operating Cost into High-Impact Restaurant Marketing Campaigns

Instead of handing used oil to informal collectors, manage it through the Biord platform and accumulate real credits. Choose the approach that best suits your business cash flow:

  • Pix withdrawals: Money paid directly into your account to cover operating expenses or payroll.
  • B2B marketplace discounts: Use your credits to subscribe to food service tools with exclusive discounts.

How to Attract More Restaurant Customers with Local Marketing

For people looking for lunch, a dinner delivery or a new place in the neighborhood, location matters. Your restaurant’s presence in the Biord network connects your offer with residents who already use cooking oil drop-off points. The goal is to turn that local connection into a first visit and give customers reasons to return.

From the First Visit to Customer Loyalty

A customer acquisition campaign works best with an easy-to-understand offer. Highlight the benefit, explain the coupon terms and make redemption clear. After the first purchase, Zupy loyalty programs and reminders help maintain relationships with people who discovered your restaurant locally.

  • Attraction: introduce your restaurant to residents near participating drop-off points and residential communities.
  • Conversion: offer a welcome coupon with clear terms and a simple redemption experience.
  • Retention: use rewards and reminders to encourage repeat visits and orders.

Restaurant Promotions: Increase Sales While Protecting Your Margin

Increasing sales does not mean giving the biggest discount on every order. Plan campaigns around your operating goals: attract new customers, bring in business during quieter hours or encourage existing customers to return. Consider ingredients, packaging and reward costs before setting each offer.

Discount Coupons, Free Items and Average Order Value

A first-purchase coupon can introduce new customers to your menu. A side dish or drink offered under defined terms can complement an order and encourage trial. Assess whether the campaign generates additional purchases and whether average order value and margin justify the reward.

For delivery, clearly explain promotion terms, your service area and how to order. Simple communication reduces questions and helps customers complete their purchase. Loyalty reminders keep your brand in mind after delivery.

How to Measure Restaurant Marketing Results

Growth needs to show up in your operating results. Compare each campaign’s performance before increasing the discount or repeating the offer. Track redemptions and orders using the records available in your business and partner tools.

  • New customers: how many people made a first purchase through the campaign.
  • Repeat business: how many customers returned after using their first coupon.
  • Average order value: revenue per order compared with the previous period.
  • Margin: the result after discounts, rewards and order costs.

Use these insights to adapt offers to your audience. Oil credits and partner software discounts help you plan marketing investment without confusing revenue with profit.

Restaurant Benefits and Marketing FAQs

How does Biord help promote my restaurant locally?

The PEV network connects restaurants with residents who drop off used cooking oil. These consumers earn points and discover available benefits at partner restaurants, creating an opportunity for local promotion.

How can I use oil credits to build customer loyalty?

Restaurants can use Biord credits to access partner tools with exclusive discounts. Zupy brings together coupons, loyalty programs and reminders on WhatsApp and digital wallets to support customer relationships.

How can I increase delivery sales without relying only on ads?

Combine promotion to nearby residents with clear offers and incentives to return. Monitor order margins and use relationships with existing customers to plan future campaigns.

Are restaurant benefits the same as consumer rewards?

No. Restaurants can withdraw their credits through Pix or use them for B2B solutions. Consumers earn points by dropping off oil at PEVs and redeem them through Zupy for coupons and free items at partner restaurants.
