Biord ecosystem

Solutions for the entire used-oil ecosystem

Biord provides digital solutions for every participant in the reverse-logistics chain for used vegetable oil, bringing data, traceability, and operations together on one platform.

Restaurants and businesses

Collection scheduling, digital receipts, and environmental compliance with SINIR and SIGOR.

Individuals and consumers

Drop-off points (PEVs) and a points system with rewards and discount coupons.

Processing companies

Centralized management, reports, collection photos, and automatic issuance of CDF and MTR documents.

Collectors

Smart routes, digital operations, and automatic receipt generation — no paperwork.

Public-sector management

Real-time data on oil disposal, environmental impact, and risk mapping across sanitation networks.

Solutions by profile

How Biord serves every link in the chain

01

Restaurants, bars, and food-service businesses

Biord simplifies used cooking oil disposal management for restaurants and commercial establishments. In just a few clicks, businesses can schedule collections, review their history, and access digital disposal receipts, supporting environmental compliance. Biord is registered with SINIR and SIGOR, providing legal assurance and waste traceability.

  • Use proper disposal as a sustainability differentiator for your brand
  • Create discount coupons to attract new customers, with part of the value funded by Biord

02

Consumers and small generators

Biord provides Voluntary Drop-off Points (PEVs) for the proper disposal of used cooking oil. Each liter dropped off earns 100 points, which can be redeemed for discount coupons at partner restaurants. This allows consumers to take an active role in reverse logistics, create a positive environmental impact, and receive real benefits.

Get started
03

Used-oil collectors

For collectors, Biord provides scheduled, smart, optimized routes that reduce logistics costs related to fuel and travel time. Operations are fully digital, while Biord’s sales team handles prospecting so collectors can focus exclusively on collection.

  • Collections recorded automatically
  • Digital receipts, no paperwork
  • Real-time route tracking
Get started
04

Processing plants and treatment companies

Treatment companies benefit from centralized digital management, bringing all collection information into a single platform with full waste traceability.

  • Complete collection reports
  • Collection photos available online
  • Real-time route monitoring
  • Automatic CDF and MTR issuance, simplifying environmental compliance
Get started
05

Public-sector management

Biord supports public-sector management with real-time data on used cooking oil disposal, enabling more efficient and preventive decision-making.

  • Volume of oil collected by region
  • Environmental impact indicators, such as CO₂ reduction and avoided water contamination
  • Mapping of areas at risk of clogs and blockages in sanitation networks

Digital infrastructure for biofuel supply chains
