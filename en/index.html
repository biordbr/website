Fornecimento nacional e internacional

UCO do Brasil com rastreabilidade para mercados nacionais e internacionais.

A Biord combina fornecimento de óleo vegetal usado com uma infraestrutura digital de rastreabilidade que preserva a origem mesmo quando diferentes coletas são consolidadas. Assim, conectamos geradores, coletores, bases e compradores em uma cadeia de custódia mais transparente, verificável e preparada para due diligence, auditorias e relacionamentos comerciais de longo prazo.

Falar com o time comercial Conhecer a tecnologia

Tecnologia desenvolvida para registrar evidências de origem, movimentações, consolidação de cargas, recebimento e formação de lotes — fortalecendo o posicionamento da Biord para quem busca used cooking oil supply, UCO from Brazil, traceable UCO supplier e fornecimento internacional com rastreabilidade.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

Biord is a digital reverse-logistics platform for used cooking oil that connects those who dispose of, collect, and process the oil, providing traceability, data, and environmental compliance throughout the process.
Biord connects businesses, individuals, and companies with partner collectors, organizing collections through smart, traceable routes with digital disposal records.
Through Biord, restaurants that maintain recurring proper disposal earn badges: Bronze after 3 months, Silver after 6, and Gold after 12. The badges are visible on the platform and serve as verifiable proof of environmental sustainability.
Each collection is recorded on the platform, making it possible to track the oil from disposal to final destination, with digital receipts, collection photos, and environmental reports.
Biord provides Voluntary Drop-off Points (PEVs). Each liter dropped off earns points that can be redeemed for discount coupons at partner restaurants.
Users earn points by dropping off oil at Biord PEVs (10 points per 100 ml). On the platform, those points can be redeemed for discount coupons at Biord Shop partner restaurants.
Biord maintains Voluntary Drop-off Points (PEVs) across São Paulo and other cities. Access the platform to find the one nearest you.

Traceable UCO for biofuels and the energy transition

Biord connects Brazilian used cooking oil with feedstock supply chains for biodiesel, hydrotreated vegetable oil (HVO) and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF). Evidence-based traceability helps buyers assess waste origin and organize sustainability data.

Documented origin

Photos, timestamps, geolocation and movement confirmations.

Mass balance

Records of inputs, outputs and balances, designed around applicable ISCC requirements.

Lower-carbon economy

Recovered UCO can support renewable fuels, circularity and emissions reductions, subject to validated pathways and methodologies.
