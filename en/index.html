Domestic and international supply

Traceable UCO from Brazil for domestic and international markets.

Biord combines used vegetable oil supply with digital traceability infrastructure that preserves origin even when different collections are consolidated. We connect generators, collectors, facilities and buyers in a more transparent, verifiable chain of custody, ready for due diligence, audits and long-term commercial relationships.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

Biord is a digital reverse-logistics platform for used cooking oil that connects those who dispose of, collect, and process the oil, providing traceability, data, and environmental compliance throughout the process.
Biord connects businesses, individuals, and companies with partner collectors, organizing collections through smart, traceable routes with digital disposal records.
Through Biord, restaurants that maintain recurring proper disposal earn badges: Bronze after 3 months, Silver after 6, and Gold after 12. The badges are visible on the platform and serve as verifiable proof of environmental sustainability.
Each collection is recorded on the platform, making it possible to track the oil from disposal to final destination, with digital receipts, collection photos, and environmental reports.
Biord provides Voluntary Drop-off Points (PEVs). Each liter dropped off earns points that can be redeemed for discount coupons at partner restaurants.
Users earn points by dropping off oil at Biord PEVs (10 points per 100 ml). On the platform, those points can be redeemed for discount coupons at Biord Shop partner restaurants.
Biord maintains Voluntary Drop-off Points (PEVs) across São Paulo and other cities. Access the platform to find the one nearest you.
