Biord for restaurants

Discover how we serve restaurants, bars, snack bars, and other food-service businesses

Biord goes far beyond oil collection. It turns an environmental liability into financial value, legal assurance, and a competitive advantage for your business.

Benefits

What changes for your restaurant

01

Simple, fast, hassle-free disposal

In just a few clicks, you can schedule used cooking oil collection, review the full history, and access digital disposal receipts. Everything is centralized, organized, and ready for audits or inspections.

02

Environmental compliance and legal assurance

Biord is registered with SINIR and SIGOR, ensuring full waste traceability and alignment with environmental regulations. Your restaurant is better protected against fines and legal risks.

03

O óleo usado vira créditos e benefícios flexíveis

Escolha dentro da plataforma o benefício que faz mais sentido para a sua operação:

  • Receba créditos na plataforma Biord pelo óleo descartado corretamente
  • Use os créditos em Pix, contratação de serviços, soluções e outros benefícios disponíveis
  • Tenha mais flexibilidade para transformar descarte correto em valor real para o negócio
04

A sustainable image that adds value to your brand

Show customers that your restaurant cares about the environment. Proper oil disposal becomes a competitive differentiator, strengthening your brand reputation and your relationship with increasingly conscious consumers.

05

More customers without increasing marketing spend

Create discount coupons on the Biord platform to attract new customers. Biord may fund part of the coupon value, helping your restaurant sell more without increasing promotional costs.

Get started
After registration

Three simple steps inside the platform

No paperwork, no bureaucracy — everything is handled directly from your business dashboard.

01 — Pix

Add your Pix key

Set up payments under Settings → Pix Key to withdraw your balance from collected oil whenever you want.

02 — Coupon

Create your discount coupon

Under Coupons → Create, set your customer-service WhatsApp number and expiration date, then activate it — ready to attract Biord Shop customers.

03 — Validation

Validate it at checkout

Confirm the customer’s phone number under Validate Coupon and apply the discount directly to the final purchase amount.
