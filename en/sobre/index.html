FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is UCO?

UCO stands for used cooking oil, a waste stream from food preparation and kitchens.

Is Biord ISCC EU certified?

The platform architecture references ISCC principles, but this does not mean Biord or any batch is ISCC EU certified. Certification must be verified independently.

Is Biord already connected to the UDB?

No active connection is being claimed. The platform was designed for future interoperability, subject to onboarding, access and official integration requirements.

Can I sell used oil?

Commercial eligibility and collection availability vary by location and generator profile. Contact us or visit the platform.
